QWERTY Flip Concept

A phone for communication and creation, not consumption. No touchscreen and nothing to swipe: a screen on the inside top, a QWERTY keyboard and joystick on the inside bottom, and a camera-style zoom slider on the side. A good camera and mics for making things; everyday speakers.

Outside

Almost nothing: one flush camera with a flash near the top of the back, and an aluminium spine over the hinge. No antenna lines: the radios use the hinge joints and the battery door seam. No screen or logo. The back of the keyboard half is the one battery door, hooked in at the hinge end and held by two flush Torx screws. Right edge: a fingerprint power button and a spring-return zoom slider. Left edge, mirroring them: a silent switch and a connection switch, and on the screen half, opposite the connection switch, the SIM and microSD tray. USB-C, a mic and a speaker grille on the base edge. Open it flat to 180° and it shoots like a normal phone.

Inside, top half

One sheet of glass over the whole face. Under it, a 3.5 in OLED display with no touch layer, 59.5 × 67.5 mm, stretched to slim 3 mm sides and equal 5.5 mm ends, with the front camera on the centre line above it. The earpiece is a slit in the top edge, not on the glass.

Inside, bottom half

A clickable low-profile joystick for moving around and selecting, between a Back key and a Menu key. Above it, two rows of four keys marked with plain shapes: talk keys (Call, Message, Contacts, Favourites) on top, make keys (Camera, Voice note, Note, one you assign) below. Below it, a full number row and a QWERTY: 44 keys with clean printed legends. The whole keyboard sits low, in the thumb zone, with near-square keys. Microphone below the keys. A slim raised frame with four soft bumpers holds the glass by its edges when closed, so the screen never touches the keys.
